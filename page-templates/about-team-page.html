About the show

Culture. Conversation. Connection.

The River Musa Show explores governance, identity, and the lived experience of moving between African and European worlds.

Each episode brings honest conversations with people who live, work and build across borders: what they learned, what surprised them, and what they would tell someone just starting out.

What we stand for

Three ideas guide every episode.

Culture

We celebrate where people come from and how it shapes where they go.

Conversation

Honest, respectful talks that go beyond headlines and easy answers.

Connection

Building bridges between people, ideas and two worlds.
