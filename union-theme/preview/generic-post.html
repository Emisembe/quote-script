    Union signs new collective bargaining agreement

    Secretariat9:00 AM

    After months of negotiations, the union and the employers' association have signed a new collective bargaining agreement covering pay, allowances and working hours for the next two years.

    What changes for members

    • A general pay increase across all grades
    • Improved house and commuter allowances
    • Clear rules on overtime and leave
    When we stand together, we win. This agreement belongs to every member who took part.

    Branch officials will hold meetings at every workplace to explain the agreement. Contact your shop steward with any questions.
