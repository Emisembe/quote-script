    Who can join?

    Membership is open to every worker employed by a member institution, whether permanent, on contract or casual.

    How to join

    1. Download and fill in the membership form.
    2. Hand it to your shop steward or branch secretary.
    3. Your subscription is deducted from your salary every month.
