EFIC Logo Guidelines · 4 / 7

Colours

The same colours as the EFIC website, letters and documents.

Navy

HEX #14213D
RGB 20, 33, 61
Main colour: the logo, headings, backgrounds

Gold

HEX #E7B165
RGB 231, 177, 101
Accent: the rule, highlights, buttons on navy

White

HEX #FFFFFF
RGB 255, 255, 255
Logo text on navy

Deep Navy

HEX #0F1A30
RGB 15, 26, 48
Darker backgrounds, footers

For printing, send the PDF files and ask the printer to match these colours and show you a proof first. Every printer converts colours slightly differently, so a proof avoids surprises.

Gold is for the rule and accents, never for text on white. Gold text on a white background is too faint to read, and white text on gold is too.

File types
