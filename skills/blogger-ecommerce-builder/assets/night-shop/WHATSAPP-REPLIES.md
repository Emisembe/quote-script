# Kreislo – ready WhatsApp replies

Save these in WhatsApp Business → Business tools → Quick replies (type `/` in a chat to use one).
Short, honest, no promises you can't keep. Replace everything in [square brackets]. Swahili versions at the end.

## First contact

**/hello** – someone writes "Hi" or asks what you do
> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from Kreislo in Gießen, Germany. We sell second-hand appliances, furniture, tools and clothes, every item checked by hand, and we ship to Kenya. What are you looking for?

**/kenya** – "Do you ship to Kenya?"
> Yes. We pack in Gießen and ship with the next shipment ([date]). You get one full price including packing and shipping before you pay, and photos or a short video of the item first. Which city are you in?

**/price** – "How much is shipping?"
> It depends on size and weight. Tell me what you want and your city, and I send you one full price today: item + packing + shipping. Nothing is added later.

## Kits

**/kits** – "What kits do you have?"
> We have three ready kits, all tested, with spares for the first year:
> 🏠 Home starter: fridge-freezer, washing machine, microwave, pots – [price] €
> 🔧 Small workshop: grinder, hammer drill, jigsaw, cordless drill, consumables – [price] €
> 🌱 Small farm: tiller, chainsaw, wheelbarrow, hand tools, spares – [price] €
> Prices are for Germany; for Kenya I add shipping and send you the full price. You can swap any item. Which one fits you?

**/swap** – "Can I change the kit?"
> Yes. Tell me what to leave out or add, and I send you the new price before you pay.

**/coop** – a group, cooperative or shop asks
> For groups we put together a bigger kit (for example 2 tillers + spares + tools) and ship it on one pallet, which lowers the shipping per item. Tell me how many people and what you farm or build, and I send a proposal within [2] days.

## New goods on request

**/new** – "Can you buy X for me in Germany?"
> Yes. Send me the product name or a link. I check the German price and whether it's allowed into Kenya, and you get one quote: shop price + our buying fee ([X] %) + shipping. We buy it only after you confirm.

## The three objections

**/local** – "It's cheaper locally"
> Locally you often buy a cheap new one for less today. But it usually lasts 3–4 years; a good German machine lasts 8–10. Over ten years you buy ours once and the local one three times. And the spares for the common faults are already in the box. Want me to compare the two for your item?

**/used** – "Why used? Will it work?"
> Every item is tested in Gießen before it's packed: appliances run a full test, tools run under load, and the test record goes in the box. You see photos or a video before you pay. If it doesn't work on arrival, [describe your rule: e.g. we replace it with the next shipment or refund the item price].

**/spares** – "What about spare parts?"
> We pack a spares bag with the parts that fail first (hoses, fuses, brushes, belts, filters). Other parts: send me the model number and I buy it new in Germany. Common parts (spark plugs, oil, discs) you get locally.

## Order and payment

**/order** – customer says yes
> Great. Here is your order: [items] – [price] € including packing and shipping to [city]. Pay by bank transfer or PayPal [or M-Pesa: your number]. Once the money is in, I send you a photo of the packed box and the shipment date.

**/paid** – payment received
> Received, thank you! Your order number is [number]. Packed by [date], shipped with the [date] shipment, expected in [city] around [date]. I'll message you when it leaves and when it arrives.

**/track** – "Where is my order?"
> Your order [number] is [packed / on the ship / arrived at the port / ready for pickup]. Next update [when]. If anything changes I tell you first.

**/nocash** – "I don't have the money now"
> No problem. Tell me the item you want and I keep the offer open until [date]. Pay when you're ready and it goes with the next shipment after that. We don't offer instalments, so you never pay interest.

## When you don't know yet

**/check** – anything you need to check first
> Good question. Let me check that properly and come back to you by [time]. I'd rather give you a right answer than a fast one.

---
Rules behind these replies: one full price before payment, no instalments, no tax advice (point to a Kenyan accountant), no promises about customs you can't keep, and "let me check" is always a good answer.
