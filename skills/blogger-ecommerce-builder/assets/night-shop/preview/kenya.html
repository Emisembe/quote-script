Shipping to Kenya

Good things from Germany, for homes and businesses in Kenya. We find, test, clean and pack second-hand goods in Gießen and ship them to Kenya. You know the full price before you pay.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need: choose from the shop or send us a request below (or on WhatsApp).
  2. We send you a price including packing and shipping to Kenya, with photos of the items.
  3. You pay by bank transfer, PayPal [or M-Pesa: your Paybill/Till number].
  4. We pack and ship with the next shipment and keep you updated on WhatsApp.
  5. You collect in [city / pickup point], or we arrange delivery [where possible].

What we ship

  • Washing machines, fridges and other appliances – tested and working
  • Furniture and household goods
  • Clothes, shoes and bags in good condition
  • Electronics – tested, with a test record
  • Tools, bicycles, baby and children's items
  • Larger orders for shops and businesses on request

We only send items that are allowed into Kenya and that work. Every electrical item is tested before it leaves Gießen.
