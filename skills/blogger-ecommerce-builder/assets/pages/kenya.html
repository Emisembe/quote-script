Need something new? We buy it for you

Some things are better new: a specific tool, a machine part, a model you've seen online. We buy it in Germany (Bauhaus, OBI, Bosch, Makita, any German shop) and ship it with your order. You get the German shop price plus our buying fee, in writing, before you pay. No stock, no surprises.

  • Send us the name or a link to the product
  • We check the price and whether it's allowed into Kenya
  • You get one quote: product + fee + shipping
  • Delivered with the next shipment

Buy once instead of three times

A good German appliance or tool usually lasts 8 to 10 years. A cheap new one often needs replacing after 3 or 4. Over ten years, a tested second-hand machine from us is usually the cheaper choice, even with shipping, and the spares are in the box.
