Emisembe Writer Desktop App – Complete Guide

Version: November 2026  ·  For: Murdock Emisembe (personal use)  ·  PC: Windows 11, Intel i5-1135G7, 32 GB RAM

This guide explains how to set up and use the Emisembe Writer desktop app on your Windows PC. The app opens in your browser (Chrome or Edge) and can paraphrase, humanise and check grammar in English and German, from level A1 to C2, in your own voice. It works with free cloud AI, paid cloud AI, or completely offline on your own computer.

1. What the desktop app is

The desktop app is a single web page (index.html) that you open from a folder on your PC. You do not need to install it. It has two panels: your text on the left and the result on the right.

  • Paraphrase – 8 styles (Standard, Fluency, Formal / Business, Friendly, Simple, Academic, Shorter, Longer) and 3 change amounts (Light, Medium, Strong).
  • Humanise – makes text sound natural and personal, removes stock phrases and clichés.
  • Grammar check – underlines errors with explanations at your level. Apply fixes one by one or all at once.
  • Level – Keep, A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2.
  • Output – Same language, English or Deutsch (translate and rewrite in one step).
  • My voice – rewrites in your personal style, in English and German.
  • History – your last 30 results are saved on your PC.

It works with any text: Google Docs, Word, emails, WhatsApp messages, applications – copy in, fix, copy out.

3. Step 1 – Download and unzip the project

  1. Save the file Emisembe-Writer.zip to your PC, for example into Documents.
  2. Right-click the zip → Extract All… → Extract.
  3. You now have a folder writer with these folders inside: app, core, google-docs, guides, tests.
  4. Important: keep this structure. The app (app/index.html) needs the file core/writer-core.js next to it. Do not move index.html out of the folder on its own.
  5. Tip: right-click app/start-writer.bat → Show more options → Send to → Desktop (create shortcut). Now you can start the app from your desktop.

4. Step 2 – Quick start with free cloud AI

2a – Get a free Gemini key

  1. Open https://aistudio.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Click Get API key → Create API key.
  3. Copy the key (it usually starts with AIza…). Keep it private.

2b – Connect the app

  1. Double-click app/start-writer.bat (or open app/index.html in Chrome or Edge).
  2. Click Settings (top right).
  3. Under Engine, choose Cloud: Google Gemini.
  4. Paste your key into Gemini key.
  5. Click Find models and choose a "flash" model (for example gemini-2.5-flash).
  6. Click Save. The status at the top right turns green: "Cloud · gemini-…".

You are ready. Paste text on the left and press Ctrl+Enter.

Privacy note: On the free tier, Google may use your text to improve its products. For private documents, use Claude or the offline engine.

2c – Later: Claude (paid, best quality)

  1. Open https://console.anthropic.com, create an account, add credit under Billing (for example $5), and set a monthly spending limit.
  2. API Keys → Create Key, copy the key (starts with sk-ant-).
  3. In the app: Settings → Engine → Cloud: Claude, paste the key, choose the model (Opus 5.5 best, Sonnet 5.5 cheaper, Haiku 5.5 cheapest), Save.

5. Step 3 – Free up disk space (for offline use)

Your C: drive shows 473 of 477 GB used – only about 4 GB free. The offline setup needs at least 8 GB (Ollama about 2 GB + the AI model about 3.3 GB + room to work). About 20 GB free is comfortable, and Windows also runs better with more free space.

Where to find space

  1. Storage overview: Start → Settings → System → Storage. Windows shows what uses the most space.
  2. Temporary files: in Storage, click Temporary files, tick the items (not "Downloads" unless you checked it), click Remove files.
  3. Cleanup recommendations: in Storage, click Cleanup recommendations – large files, unused apps, files synced to the cloud.
  4. Downloads folder: open it, sort by Size, delete what you no longer need (old installers, videos, zips).
  5. Recycle Bin: right-click → Empty Recycle Bin.
  6. Apps: Settings → Apps → Installed apps, sort by size, uninstall what you do not use (games, old programs).
  7. Videos and photos: move them to an external drive or Google Drive (then delete them from the PC).
  8. Camtasia / video projects: old project and recording files can be very large – archive them to an external drive.

Alternative: keep the AI models on an external drive (see Section 15, "Move the models").

6. Step 4 – Set up offline AI with Ollama

Ollama is a free program that runs AI models on your own PC. Nothing you write leaves your computer.

4a – Run the setup file

  1. Open the folder writer/app.
  2. Double-click setup-windows.bat. A black window opens.
  3. It checks your free disk space. If there are less than 8 GB, it stops and tells you – free up more space first (Step 3).
  4. If Ollama is not installed, it opens the download page https://ollama.com/download. Download Ollama for Windows, install it (Next → Install), then double-click setup-windows.bat again.
  5. The setup allows the app to talk to Ollama, then downloads the model gemma3:4b (about 3.3 GB). This can take 5–30 minutes depending on your internet.
  6. When it says "Almost done!", press any key to close the window.

4b – Restart Ollama (required once)

  1. Look at the bottom right of the screen, near the clock. Click the small arrow ^ if needed.
  2. Right-click the llama icon → Quit Ollama.
  3. Open the Start menu, type Ollama, and start it again.

4c – Switch the app to offline

  1. Start the app (start-writer.bat).
  2. Settings → Engine → Offline: Ollama on this PC.
  3. Offline model: choose gemma3:4b (click Refresh if the list is empty).
  4. Save. The status at the top right shows a green dot: "Offline · gemma3:4b".

Speed: your PC runs the model on the processor (the MX450 graphics card is too small to help much). Expect about 10–30 seconds per paragraph. The first request after starting can take longer, because the model is loaded into memory. The text appears word by word while it is written – you can click Stop at any time.

Better quality (optional, slower)

With 32 GB RAM you can also run a bigger model. Open the Start menu, type cmd, open Command Prompt, and type:

ollama pull gemma3:12b

It needs about 8 GB more disk space and is about 3 times slower. Choose it in Settings → Offline model. You can switch between the models at any time: 4b for quick work, 12b for important texts.

7. Step 5 (optional) – Offline grammar with LanguageTool

LanguageTool is a dedicated grammar checker for English and German. It is very fast and fully offline. Its explanations are not adapted to your level (the AI checker does that).

  1. Install Java (version 17 or newer) from https://adoptium.net (choose "Temurin", Windows, .msi installer).
  2. Download https://languagetool.org/download/LanguageTool-stable.zip (about 250 MB).
  3. Unzip it into the folder writer/app. You get a folder like LanguageTool-6.x inside app.
  4. Double-click start-languagetool.bat. A window opens and says it is starting on port 8081. Keep this window open while you work; close it to stop LanguageTool.
  5. In the app: Settings → Checker → LanguageTool on this PC → Save.

Without installing anything, you can also choose LanguageTool online (free, but the text is sent to languagetool.org).

9. How to use each tool

Paraphrase

  1. Paste your text on the left.
  2. Click the Paraphrase tab, choose a Style, a Level, the Output language and the Change amount. Switch on My voice if you want it to sound like you.
  3. Press Ctrl+Enter (or click Rephrase).
  4. Read the result. Not happy? Change an option and run again, or click Use as input and run another style on top.
  5. Click Copy and paste the result where you need it.
StyleBest for
StandardSame message in fresh words.
FluencyFix awkward or spoken-style sentences with minimal changes.
Formal / BusinessEmails to companies, offices, customers, applications.
FriendlyWarm messages to colleagues and friends.
SimpleEasy-to-understand text, short sentences.
AcademicReports, essays, research.
ShorterAbout 30–50% shorter, same points.
LongerFuller explanation of the same points (no new facts).

Humanise

Use it when text sounds stiff or "too perfect". It mixes short and long sentences, uses natural words and contractions, and removes stock phrases like "I hope this message finds you well". It keeps every fact, name and number. Best together with My voice.

Honest note: no tool can guarantee that "AI detectors" will not flag a text; detectors are unreliable.

Grammar check

  1. Click the Grammar check tab. Choose the Text language (Auto, English, Deutsch) and the Level for the explanations.
  2. Press Ctrl+Enter (or click Check).
  3. Errors are underlined in red. Click an underline to jump to its card in the list below.
  4. Each card shows the error (crossed out), the correction (green) and an explanation. Click Apply to fix it, or Ignore to keep it.
  5. Apply all fixes everything at once. Fixed parts turn green.
  6. Click Copy to copy the corrected text.

Translate while rewriting

Set Output to English or Deutsch. Example: a rough English note → Paraphrase · Formal · Output Deutsch · B2 → a clean, formal German email. (Change highlighting is switched off when the language changes, because every word is new.)

Language levels

LevelResult
A1Very short sentences, very common words. German: main clauses, Präsens, no Genitiv/Konjunktiv/Passiv.
A2Short clear sentences, everyday words. German: simple weil/dass/wenn clauses, Perfekt.
B1Clear and straightforward, some linking words. German: subordinate clauses, simple Konjunktiv II.
B2Natural and fluent, wider vocabulary. German: Passiv, Konjunktiv II.
C1Precise and well-structured, complex sentences. German: Nominalstil, Konjunktiv I.
C2Near-native and refined.
KeepSame level as your original.

11. History, shortcuts and handy buttons

  • Ctrl+Enter – run the current tool.
  • History – shows your last 30 results with date and options. Click one to load it back. Clear history deletes them.
  • Use as input – chain tools, for example Grammar → Apply all → Use as input → Humanise with My voice.
  • Stop – cancels a slow offline run.
  • Your draft on the left is saved automatically; it is still there after you close and reopen the app.

12. Tips for the best results

  • Work on a few paragraphs at a time, especially offline. It is faster and better.
  • For rough voice-note style drafts: Paraphrase · Fluency · Light first, then Humanise + My voice.
  • For applications and official letters: Paraphrase · Formal · B2 or C1 · My voice, then a final Grammar check.
  • For German learners or simple messages: choose A2 or B1.
  • Add real German samples to My voice – it then sounds like you in German too.
  • Always read the result before you send it. The AI keeps facts and numbers, but you are the final check.

14. Privacy, security and costs

  • Offline (Ollama) and LanguageTool on this PC: free; your text never leaves your computer.
  • Gemini free tier: free; text goes to Google and may be used to improve its products.
  • Claude: paid per use (about 1–2 cents per paragraph with Opus 5.5, less with Sonnet or Haiku); text goes to Anthropic to be processed. Set a monthly spending limit in the console.
  • LanguageTool online: free; text goes to languagetool.org.
  • Your keys are stored only in this browser on your PC.
  • About the setup setting: setup-windows.bat sets OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* so the app (opened as a local file) can talk to Ollama. While Ollama runs, web pages in your browser could also send requests to it – they can use your model, but they cannot read your files. To undo it, open Command Prompt, run setx OLLAMA_ORIGINS "", and restart Ollama.

15. Updating, moving and uninstalling

Update the app

Unzip the new version over the old folder (replace files). Your settings, keys and history stay, because they are stored in the browser – as long as the folder path stays the same.

Move the models to another drive

  1. Quit Ollama.
  2. Open Command Prompt and run (example for drive D): setx OLLAMA_MODELS "D:\ollama-models"
  3. Start Ollama again and download the model again: ollama pull gemma3:4b
  4. Delete the old models folder: C:\Users\(your name)\.ollama\models

Remove a model

ollama rm gemma3:12b (frees its disk space).

Uninstall everything

  • Ollama: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Ollama → Uninstall.
  • Delete the writer folder.
  • Optional: setx OLLAMA_ORIGINS ""
