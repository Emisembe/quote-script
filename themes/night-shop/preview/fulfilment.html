Fulfilment for sellers

You sell. We store, pack and ship. Kreislo fulfilment from Gießen, in the middle of Germany, for online shops, marketplace sellers and small brands.

What we do for you

  • Storage on shelves or pallets
  • Picking and packing, with your flyers or gifts
  • Shipping with DHL, Hermes or DPD, with tracking
  • Returns: received, checked and put back in stock
  • Same-day dispatch for orders received by [12:00]
  • One personal contact on WhatsApp, phone or email

How it works

  1. Request a quote with the form below. We answer within one working day.
  2. We agree your prices: storage, per order, packaging. No hidden fees.
  3. Send us your stock. We count it in and confirm it the same day.
  4. Send us your orders (email, list or shop export). We pack and ship, you get the tracking numbers.
