The Future of Artificial Intelligence in Everyday Life

By Jane Doe, Technology Editor ● Thursday, 20 May 2026 ● 8 min read ● 14 Comments

Artificial intelligence is no longer confined to research labs or science-fiction films. From the smartphone in your pocket to the recommendations on your favourite streaming platform, AI is quietly reshaping the way we live, work, and connect with one another. But what does the next decade hold, and how should we prepare for a world increasingly guided by machine intelligence?

The answer lies not in fear or blind optimism, but in understanding — and that's exactly what this investigation aims to provide. We spoke to leading researchers, policymakers, and everyday users to build a picture of the AI-integrated world that is already, in many ways, here.

"The workers who thrive will be those who treat AI as a collaborator rather than a competitor."

1. AI in the Home

Smart speakers, robotic vacuum cleaners, and AI-powered thermostats are already common fixtures in millions of homes. The next generation goes further: predictive appliances that learn your schedule, health monitors that flag anomalies before symptoms appear, and ambient assistants that respond to context rather than commands.

By 2028, analysts at Gartner estimate that more than 60% of new household appliances sold in OECD countries will include embedded AI capabilities — a figure that would have seemed implausible just five years ago.

2. AI at Work

The World Economic Forum estimates that AI will displace 85 million jobs by 2025 — but create 97 million new ones. The shift is already visible: legal assistants, radiologists, and financial analysts are augmenting their work with AI tools that handle volume while humans handle nuance and judgment.

The key skill for the next decade is not technical — it's adaptability. The workers who thrive will be those who treat AI as a collaborator rather than a competitor, learning to direct, verify, and extend what the machines produce.
