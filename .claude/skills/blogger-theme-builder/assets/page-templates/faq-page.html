Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the show, our content and how to get involved. Tap a question to see the answer.

About the show

What is The River Musa Show about?

We explore governance, identity, and the lived experience of moving between African and European worlds, through honest conversations with people who have lived it.

How often do new episodes come out?

A new episode is published every Friday. Join the newsletter or follow us on YouTube to get it first.

Where can I listen or watch?

On this website, on YouTube, and on the podcast platforms listed under "Listen on" on the homepage.

Getting involved

Can I be a guest on the show?

Yes! We're always looking for people with a real story. See the Be a Guest page and apply there.

Can I suggest a topic?

Please do. Send your idea through the Contact page or a WhatsApp message.

How can I support the show?

Share episodes with friends, leave a comment, subscribe on YouTube, or use the "Support the Show" button at the bottom of every page. Every bit helps us keep going.

Downloads & content

Are the downloads really free?

Yes. All guides and checklists on the Downloads page are free to use and share. Please don't sell them.

Can I use clips from the show?

Short clips with credit and a link back are welcome for non-commercial use. For anything else, please ask us first.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Get in touch and we'll reply as soon as we can.
