Cross-border careers: building a life between Nairobi and Berlin

EficOctober 12, 2026

Moving between two worlds is rarely a single decision. It is a series of small negotiations: with employers, with family, with yourself. In this episode we unpack what it really takes.

What nobody tells you

Qualifications travel less easily than people. Recognition of credentials is often the first wall, and the second is the unwritten rules of a new workplace.

"You are always translating, even when everyone speaks the same language."
  • Document everything before you move.
  • Find one mentor on each side.
  • Keep a foot in both networks.

The riverbank is where the two worlds meet, and it is a good place to build from.
